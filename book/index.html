Leading Change

Most change does not fail because the idea was bad. It stalls because people were not ready, their concerns were treated as obstacles, or the effort ran out of energy after launch. This chapter gives you the classic models, an honest view of their limits, and practical tools for building readiness and making change stick.

  • What change asks of people
  • Lewin's foundations: fields, forces and three steps
  • Kotter's eight steps
  • Readiness for change
  • Resistance as information
